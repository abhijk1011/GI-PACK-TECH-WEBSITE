## Where pallets get damaged

A pallet is where a shipment is most exposed. The goods have left their individual cartons behind, they are stacked in the open, and they will be handled several times by people who did not pack them. Damage on the pallet is the most common damage in the supply chain — and the cheapest to prevent.

The protection a pallet needs depends on three things: what threatens it (water, dust, UV, load collapse, corrosion), how long it is exposed, and whether it is covered once or repeatedly.

## Stretch wrap leaves the top open

Stretch wrap is excellent at one job — holding a stack together. Wound around the sides, it leaves the top face of the pallet open, and that is exactly where dust settles and water lands first. When the top layer of cartons arrives dusty or water-stained, this is usually why.

The cheapest fix is a **top sheet**: a sheet of LDPE laid over the stack before it is wrapped. A **pallet liner** laid on the deck under the load does the same for the bottom layer, stopping moisture, splinters and nail heads from the pallet reaching the goods.

## Pallet hoods: top and sides in one piece

An LDPE pallet hood is pulled down over the loaded pallet like a bag. It covers the top and all four sides with no joins for water or dust to get through. It must be made to the pallet size and stack height — a standard size is either a struggle to pull down or so loose it flaps and tears.

## Shrink hoods: one solid block

A shrink hood is pulled over the load and shrunk with heat so it grips the stack tightly. The result is a sealed, weather-tight unit that behaves like one solid block in handling. When a load collapses in transit the whole pallet is a loss, not just one carton, so shrink hoods are used for export, open transport, outdoor storage, building materials and heavy or unstable loads. The film must shrink enough to grip without splitting at the corners, so shrink ratio and thickness are worked out against the load.

## Reusable covers: buy once

For pallets that are stored outdoors for long periods, or opened for picking and re-covered, a single-use cover is bought again and again. A **woven laminated cover** has a tear strength film cannot reach and is weatherproof; a **PVC-coated fabric cover** is built for daily use over years. The fittings — eyelets, tie-downs, closures, handles — decide whether a reusable cover actually gets reused. A cover that takes two people to fit will be left off.

## VCI pallet covers: corrosion in one step

Wrapping metal parts one by one is slow, and oiling them means degreasing them later. A VCI pallet cover protects a whole pallet of machined parts, castings or fasteners in one operation: the inhibitor vapour fills the space under the cover and protects every metal surface inside, with nothing to clean off.
