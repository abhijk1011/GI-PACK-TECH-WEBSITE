## Why export machinery corrodes

A machine leaves the assembly floor clean and accurate. Several weeks later — after a truck, a port, a sea voyage, another port and an inland journey — it is uncrated on the customer's site. In between it has been exposed to humid air, salt, temperature swings and [condensation inside the container](/blog/container-rain-and-cargo-sweat).

The damage is found at commissioning: a corroded slideway, rust on a ground surface, moisture in a control cabinet. By then the manufacturer's engineer is on site, the customer is waiting, and the cost is not the repair. It is the delay, the travel, the penalty clause and the reputation. A machine tool is sold on its accuracy, and a corroded slideway cannot be polished back to it.

## A crate is not a barrier

A wooden crate or skid protects a machine from knocks and makes it liftable. It does nothing about moisture: air, and the moisture in it, passes straight through. Wood itself carries moisture into the pack. Corrosion protection has to come from a sealed barrier **around the machine**, inside the crate.

## The four parts of a seaworthy pack

| Part | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Aluminium foil laminate cover and base | Stops moisture, salt air and oxygen getting in — near-total barrier |
| Desiccant | Absorbs the moisture trapped inside at sealing, and any that permeates through the seals over the voyage |
| VCI | Protects bare metal surfaces — including ones the film never touches |
| Vacuum drawing | Removes most of the air and moisture before the final seal; pulls the cover tight |

### The barrier

An [aluminium vacuum laminate cover](/products/machine-covers-and-protective-packaging/aluminium-vacuum-laminate-cover) — aluminium foil laminated with nylon and polyester, in up to six layers — gives near-total protection against moisture and gas, with very little passing even at the heat seal. The cover is fabricated to the machine and sealed to a barrier base sheet, so the machine sits in a closed envelope.

### The desiccant

Desiccant is placed inside before sealing. The quantity depends on the enclosed volume, the barrier's water vapour transmission, the area of the seals, the length of the voyage and anything hygroscopic inside the pack — such as wood. Barrier packs are commonly designed to hold the air inside below about 40 per cent relative humidity, where steel corrosion is very slow. A **humidity indicator** card visible through a window lets the receiving engineer see that the pack held.

### VCI

A VCI inner layer releases a corrosion-inhibiting vapour inside the sealed cover that settles on every metal surface — bores, gear cases, threaded holes — with nothing to clean off before use. See [what is VCI packaging](/blog/what-is-vci-packaging).

### Vacuum

Drawing the air out before the final seal removes most of the moisture and oxygen, holds the cover tight against the machine so it does not chafe, and makes a leak visible.

## The seal decides it

Intact aluminium foil stops almost everything. The weak points are the **heat seals** and any **punctures** — from sharp corners, lifting points or handling. That is why the seal specification, and reinforcement where forks and slings touch, matter more than the layer count. ## Built to the machine drawing

There is no standard size in machinery. Every cover is fabricated from the **general arrangement drawing**, including projections — control panels, exhausts, spindles, conveyors. A cover that is too loose leaves slack that traps water and tears at the corners; one built to the profile seals properly. GI PackTech makes covers for:

## For OEMs shipping a product line

Machinery manufacturers who ship the same models repeatedly can standardise: one cover specification per model, made from the drawing once and repeated on every order, with the manufacturer's identification printed or labelled where needed. A trial cover on one machine proves the fit before the specification is fixed.

## What to send

- The general arrangement drawing, with projections and lifting points
- Route, voyage length and expected storage before installation
- Metals exposed — steel, cast iron, copper, aluminium — for the VCI formulation
- Whether the machine is crated, skidded or containerised

Send one machine as a trial; the cover, desiccant and VCI are specified together for its voyage.
