## Why the liner matters more than the bag

Bulk bags are usually bought on price, and the woven polypropylene bag is the part buyers compare. But the bag's job is mechanical — to lift and carry the load. The liner inside it decides everything that touches the product: the barrier against moisture and oxygen, the static behaviour, the hygiene, and how much residue is left behind.

That is why GI PackTech specifies the liner against the product, not against the bag.

## When an FIBC needs a liner

- **Fine powders** that would sift through the weave
- **Moisture-sensitive** products — hygroscopic powders, fertilisers, cement additives
- **Oxygen-sensitive** products — food ingredients, oils, flavours, some chemicals
- **Hygiene** — food and pharmaceutical products that must not touch the fabric
- **Static** — flammable powders, where a conductive liner is part of a static-safe system
- **Aggressive chemicals** that need a specific contact film

## Liner shapes

| Shape | What it is | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loose tube | A tube of film inserted into the bag, often with a top and bottom spout | General use, lowest cost, easy to change |
| Form-fit | Shaped to the bag's interior, spouts included, usually attached to the bag | Complete discharge, less residue, automated filling |
| Baffle (gambo) | Internal baffles hold the liner square as it fills | Stacking, container fill, square loads with a barrier |
| Box type | Pre-formed square profile that opens square in the bag | Faster filling, fewer creases, full use of volume |

**Loose tube** liners are the simplest and most widely used. **Form-fit** liners follow the bag's shape so product is not trapped in folds and the liner is not drawn into the discharge spout. A [box type liner](/products/bulk-bag-and-fibc-liners/pe-box-type-liner) opens square from the first load, which speeds filling and reduces creasing. A [baffle and gambo liner](/products/bulk-bag-and-fibc-liners/baffle-and-gambo-liner) brings the shape control of a baffle bag down to liner level.

## Combining shape and material

Most suppliers offer box type and baffle liners only in plain polyethylene, which forces a choice between shape and barrier or static protection. That choice is not necessary. GI PackTech builds EVOH into box type and baffle constructions, and makes box type liners in conductive film as well — so a food ingredient can have an oxygen barrier **and** a square, stackable load, and a flammable powder can have static protection **and** fast, crease-free filling.

Building baffles into barrier and conductive films is difficult, because every baffle joint is a potential weak point in the barrier. Solving that is what separates a specialised liner from a commodity one.

## How to choose

1. **What threatens the product?** Moisture → aluminium. Oxygen → EVOH. Static → conductive. Nothing beyond contamination → multilayer PE.
2. **How is it filled?** Automated or high-speed filling favours form-fit or box type liners.
3. **How is it stored and shipped?** Stacking and container fill favour box type or baffle shapes — see [baffle bags vs round FIBCs](/blog/baffle-bags-vs-round-fibc).
4. **How is it discharged?** Complete discharge favours a form-fit liner with a matched bottom spout.
5. **Is the bag static-protected?** If so, the liner must match it — see [FIBC types A, B, C and D](/blog/fibc-types-a-b-c-d-explained).
