## The problem VCI solves

Machined steel starts to rust as soon as moisture and oxygen reach it. The traditional answer is to coat every surface in oil, grease or a rust-preventive compound — which works, but every gram has to be cleaned off before the part is used, usually with solvent, by someone on the customer's site. Coatings also miss what they cannot reach: internal bores, gear cases, blind threaded holes.

**VCI** — volatile corrosion inhibitor, sometimes called vapour corrosion inhibitor — protects without a coating.

## How it works

1. The VCI film, bag, cover or paper is loaded with an inhibitor compound that slowly **vaporises** at room temperature.
2. Inside a **closed enclosure**, the vapour spreads through the air and reaches every metal surface — including surfaces the film never touches.
3. It **adsorbs** onto the metal as an extremely thin layer that interferes with the corrosion reaction.
4. When the part is unwrapped, the layer **dissipates**. There is nothing to clean off.

## When VCI works — and when it does not

| VCI works well when | VCI struggles when |
| --- | --- |
| The enclosure is closed and holds the vapour | The cover is open, torn or loosely tied |
| The formulation matches the metal | A ferrous formulation is used on copper alloys |
| Parts go in clean and dry | Parts are wet, salty or carry fingerprints |
| The VCI quantity suits the enclosed volume | A small VCI bag is expected to protect a large space |
| The storage period is matched to the pack | Long, humid voyages rely on VCI film alone |

The most common failure is not the chemistry but the enclosure. VCI vapour that escapes protects nothing, so the **cover seal and the VCI loading are designed together** — on a [screw compressor cover](/products/machine-covers-and-protective-packaging/screw-compressor-cover), a pallet cover or a machine cover alike.

## Match the formulation to the metal

Inhibitors are metal-specific. A formulation for steel and cast iron does little for copper, brass or bronze, and an assembly with several metals needs a multi-metal formulation. Using the wrong one does nothing at all. Tell the supplier every metal present.

## VCI formats

- **[VCI machine covers](/products/machine-covers-and-protective-packaging/vci-machine-cover)** — VCI polyethylene film, VCI paper, or VCI combined with aluminium laminate, made to the machine or component.
- **[VCI pallet covers](/products/pallet-covers-and-pallet-wraps/vci-pallet-cover)** — a whole pallet of machined parts, castings, forgings or fasteners protected in one operation instead of wrapping each part.
- **VCI bags, sheets and interleaving** — for individual parts, layers in a crate and spares in long storage.
- **VCI inner layer on a barrier cover** — on an [aluminium vacuum laminate cover](/products/machine-covers-and-protective-packaging/aluminium-vacuum-laminate-cover), for long voyages.

## VCI and barrier together

VCI film is a corrosion inhibitor, not a moisture barrier. On a short, dry route that is enough. On a long sea voyage, through humid ports or into months of site storage, combine VCI with a sealed **aluminium barrier and desiccant**: the barrier keeps moisture out, the desiccant dries the air inside, and the VCI protects the metal from whatever remains. The [seaworthy export packing guide](/blog/seaworthy-export-packing-for-machinery) sets out the method.

## Where VCI is used

Machined components, gearboxes, engine parts, bearings, tooling, dies and moulds, castings and forgings, fasteners, automotive parts, and spare parts in long storage.

## How it is tested

Buyers who need evidence often specify a laboratory test of the VCI material's inhibiting ability, such as NACE TM0208 for ferrous metals. Ask for the test and the formulation that matches your metals at enquiry stage.

Send the parts or assembly, the metals, how they are packed now and the storage or transit period; the VCI type and the enclosure are specified together.
