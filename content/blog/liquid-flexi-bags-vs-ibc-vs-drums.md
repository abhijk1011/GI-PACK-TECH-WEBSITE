## Three ways to move a tonne of liquid

For roughly a tonne of liquid, there are three common formats:

- **Drums** — about five 200-litre drums, handled individually
- **A rigid IBC** — a 1,000-litre plastic bottle in a steel cage, on its own pallet
- **A liquid flexi bag** — a 1,000 to 1,500-litre flexible bag in an outer holding assembly

Each one is right somewhere. The difference is in what happens after the liquid is delivered.

## The hidden cost of an IBC fleet

An IBC is an asset you buy once and then pay for forever. Between fills it has to be cleaned. It has to be tracked across customers, inspected and repaired. Empties take up space at the customer's site and travel back on a truck carrying nothing. When a route is long, one-way or across a border, a large share of the IBCs simply never come back.

None of that cost appears on the price of the IBC. Most operations have never added it up.

## How a flexi bag works

A [liquid flexi bag](/products/liquid-flexi-bags-and-bladder-tanks/liquid-flexi-bag) arrives flat. It is placed in its outer holding assembly, filled through the inlet valve, carried, emptied through the discharge valve, and then disposed of or recycled. There is no container to clean, track or bring back.

The **holding assembly** is the part that decides whether it works. A flexible bag with nothing holding it in shape is unusable in practice — this is where flexi bag installations usually fail. GI PackTech builds the outer assembly as part of the product rather than leaving the buyer to find one.

## Where each one is right

- **Drums** suit small lots, many different products, and products drawn off a little at a time. A [drum liner with an integrated dispensing spout](/products/round-drum-liners/drum-liner-with-integrated-dispensing-spout) keeps the drum clean and reusable and the batch sealed between draws.
- **IBCs** suit a short, closed loop between two sites where the empties reliably come back and cleaning is cheap.
- **Flexi bags** suit one-way routes, export, customers who do not want to store or return containers, and any operation where cleaning and returns are the larger cost.

## What flexi bags carry

Non-hazardous liquid chemicals, edible oils, water-based products, liquid food ingredients and industrial fluids, in multilayer polyethylene film. Barrier and conductive constructions are available on request where the liquid needs them. For hazardous liquids, the packaging must meet the transport rules for the product, so give the classification at enquiry.

## On site: storage and spills

For site storage of water, fuel or process liquid on construction projects, shutdowns and remote sites, a [collapsible bladder tank](/products/liquid-flexi-bags-and-bladder-tanks/collapsible-bladder-tank) with its own knock-down support frame goes up in hours and moves to the next site. Under any liquid storage, a [flexible spill bund](/products/hazardous-waste-containment/spill-containment-bund-and-drip-tray) catches leaks.

Send the liquid, its data sheet, volume per unit, how it is filled and emptied, and the route; the bag, valves and holding assembly are specified together.
